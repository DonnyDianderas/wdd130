Our Top Rafting Trips

Urubamba River Adventure - Sacred Valley

Moderate (Class II and III) - Full day. Enjoy scenic views of the Sacred Valley, suitable for beginners and families, with a riverside Andean lunch included.

Apurímac River Expedition - Deep Canyon Rapids

High difficulty (Class III and IV) - 3 days. Face intense rapids, camp in remote canyons, and spot Andean condors. Perfect for experienced rafters.

Marañón River Expedition - Amazonian Rapids

Moderate (Class II and III) - 5 days. Experience a mix of rapids and calm stretches with rainforest views and interactions with indigenous communities. Great for intermediate adventurers.
